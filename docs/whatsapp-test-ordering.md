# Pipeline 03D — WhatsApp test customer ordering
Only signed, allowlisted test senders with a linked customer account. Send SHOP (or SHOP 2), BUY 1 2 for item 1 and 2 kg, then CONFIRM followed by the exact UUID from the quote. Quotes expire in 10 minutes, do not reserve stock; confirm is atomic through shared checkout, no duplicate order on replay. TRACK or TRACK full-order-UUID shows own test order status. RESET discards draft only. One item per order, fixed demo delivery address, no charges or real delivery. Cancellation of confirmed orders in website My orders. Unlink removes draft. A lost reply does not imply the order failed: use TRACK or website. Temporary token can expire. No production messaging.
